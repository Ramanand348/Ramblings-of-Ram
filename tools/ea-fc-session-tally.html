EA FC Session Tally

A tap-friendly counter for the problems you hit while playing. Everything stays on your device, and you can export it whenever you want a record.

Tool Gaming Stored on your device

Log a session as you play: tap + each time a problem happens, then save when you finish. Over weeks, the saved sessions turn a feeling into a count that can be checked, with dates, modes and totals. The related research piece explains why this kind of record matters: Scripting, Engagement, and the Limits of Skill in EA Sports FC.

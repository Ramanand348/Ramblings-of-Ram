What If

Take one of the world’s open mysteries, grant a plausible hypothesis as fact, and follow it as far as the evidence goes.

What If pieces

Each piece picks a hypothesis that serious researchers take seriously, grants it as true, and asks what it would explain, what it would cost, and what would settle it. The aim is the best case for the idea, made honestly, with its open edges named.

About

I'm a biotechnologist by training and an environmental engineer by degree, and Retrocalculated is where that background actually gets used.

The site holds two kinds of writing that don't obviously belong together. One half is ancient Indian history: how confident we should actually be about a Mahabharata war date, what a set of eleventh-century inscriptions really support about a naval campaign against Srivijaya, that kind of thing. The other half is engineering and biotechnology, closer to my actual training: how efficiently a Chennai desalination plant runs against the thermodynamic minimum, whether fungal dye-degradation research has ever left the lab bench for an actual textile cluster, why anaemia in India got worse after a program aimed directly at fixing it.

What ties them together isn't the subject matter. It's the standard. A claim from five thousand years ago and a claim from a government press release both deserve the same question: what's actually being said here, and does the evidence support it. Most of what gets written about either kind of claim doesn't ask that question very hard. This site is where I try to.

Two sections, held to the same standard

Research pieces are long-form and take months. They go through a full literature pass, cite everything, and, where the underlying data allows it, build original models rather than just summarizing what's already published, an actual scale-up feasibility calculation, a from-first-principles thermodynamic derivation, that kind of thing.

Notebook posts are shorter and don't need months. They still get fact-checked and cited properly. They just don't carry the full apparatus of a research paper.

Background

B.Tech in Biotechnology, M.Tech in Environmental Engineering. The site's history pieces are a personal interest pursued with the same rigor I'd want from a lab report; the science pieces are closer to home ground. Geographic scope defaults to India, and often specifically Tamil Nadu, because that's the context I actually know well enough to write about carefully.

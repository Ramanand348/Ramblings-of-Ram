About

I write about whatever I've gotten curious enough about to spend real time on, and this is where it all lives.

This site holds long-form research and shorter notes on whatever I've gotten curious enough about to work through properly. That's turned out to be a fairly wide spread: ancient Indian history, biotechnology, environmental engineering, public health, and the occasional piece that doesn't fit neatly into any of those. There isn't a single topic this site is "about." It's organized around the writing itself, not a subject.

What does stay consistent is how each piece gets built. Sources get read and cited properly. Numbers get checked, and where nobody's already worked them out, I try to work them out myself rather than just summarize what's out there. A piece only goes up once I'm confident it holds together, whatever the subject happens to be.

Research and Notebook

Research pieces are long-form and take months. They go through a full literature pass, cite everything, and, where the underlying data allows it, build original models rather than just summarizing what's already published, an actual scale-up feasibility calculation, a from-first-principles thermodynamic derivation, that kind of thing.

Notebook posts are shorter and don't need months. They still get fact-checked and cited properly. They just don't carry the full apparatus of a research paper.

Background

B.Tech in Biotechnology, M.Tech in Environmental Engineering. That background shows up directly in a lot of the science writing here, and shows up more generally as a standard of care in everything else, including the history pieces, which are a personal interest rather than a professional one. Geographic scope defaults to India, and often specifically Tamil Nadu, because that's the context I know well enough to write about carefully.

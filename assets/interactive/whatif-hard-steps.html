Gaps follow the standard hard-steps result: given that every step finishes inside the window, each step, and the time left at the end, averages the window divided by the steps plus one. The two clues are the ones used by Hanson and colleagues (2021) as an illustration. They point to different counts, which is why the number is quoted as a range.
